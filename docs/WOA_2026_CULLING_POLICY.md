Source programme: `/home/edward/Bilder/woa-2026-all-days.pdf`. Keep the PDF as
the authority for the day, stage, artist and set time; do not infer an artist
from a filename or from capture time alone.

## Export layout

Put final renders below the capture day, not below the camera:

```text
<export-root>/<día>/Fans/
<export-root>/<día>/Bandas/<artista> - <escenario>/
```

Keep fisheye images when technically usable, even if they would normally be
collapsed as a near-duplicate.

## Bands from Wednesday onward

Use the programme's set time and stage to attach each accepted photo to its
artist. Select 6–15 final images for a band: start at 6, add only meaningfully
different, technically sound frames, and stop at 15. Do not fill a quota when
there are fewer good frames. Keep a band’s fans/audience photos in `Fans`
unless the performer is clearly the primary subject.
